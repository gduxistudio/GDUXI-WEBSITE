Privacy policy

We only collect what you choose to send us, and we only use it to reply to you. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who we are

GDUXI Studio is a brand and digital design studio in the Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada. This policy covers our website at gduxi.studio and our Android app, GDUXI Studio, which shows the same website. If you have any question about your information, email us at gduxistudio@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive what you type: your name, your email address, the service you're interested in, your budget and timeline if you pick them, and your message.

When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

That's all. We don't ask you to make an account, we don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use it

  • To read your enquiry and reply to you.
  • To talk with you about a project you asked about, and to send quotes or proposals you request.

We won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing unless you ask us to.

Services that help us run the site

A few trusted services handle information on our behalf, only as needed to run the site:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes contact form messages to our email.
  • Google (Gmail) stores the messages we receive.
  • Netlify hosts the website. Like any web host, it briefly keeps technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the site secure and running.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Figma shows our design prototypes. It only loads, with its own cookies, when you open a prototype.

Some of these services store data outside Canada, including in the United States. Each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

Our site doesn't set tracking cookies. It saves one small setting on your device, your light or dark theme choice, so the site looks the way you left it. Figma may set its own cookies when you open a prototype.

The Android app

The GDUXI Studio app shows our website inside the app. It doesn't collect anything beyond what the website does. It has no ads, no analytics and no account. It asks only for internet access and to check whether you're online. Links to other sites, like LinkedIn or Figma, open in your own browser or apps, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and emails for as long as we need them to reply to you and to keep normal business records, usually no more than two years after we last hear from you. If we work together, we keep project records as long as tax and business rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email gduxistudio@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA). If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (opens in a new tab).

Children

Our site and app are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

The site and the app only use secure (HTTPS) connections, so what you send is encrypted on its way to us. Only the studio team reads your messages.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions?

Email gduxistudio@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.
